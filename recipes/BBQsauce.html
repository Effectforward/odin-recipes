Homemade BBQ Sauce

Description

if you're looking for a classic homemade BBQ sauce that's easy to make, look no further! This recipe has the perfect amount of sweetness and spice. It is very popular with users of 'ceramic' type smokers and grills, (i.e. Primo, Kamado, Big Green Egg, etc.).

Ingredients

  • 3 cups brown sugar
  • 3 cups ketchup
  • 1 cup red wine vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
  • 5 tablespoons dry mustard
  • 4 teaspoons paprika
  • 4 teaspoons salt
  • 1 tablespoon black pepper
  • 4 dashes hot pepper sauce

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Combine brown sugar, ketchup, vinegar, water, and Worcestershire sauce in a blender. Season with mustard, paprika, salt, pepper, and hot pepper sauce. Blend until smooth.
  3. Enjoy!
